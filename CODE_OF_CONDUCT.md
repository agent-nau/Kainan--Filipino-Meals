# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in the Kainan project a welcoming and respectful experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, respectful, and constructive.
- Welcome differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept feedback graciously.
- Focus on what is best for the community.
- Respect other contributors' privacy.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, intimidation, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the repository hosting platform. Do not post personal or sensitive details in a public issue.

Maintainers will review reports fairly and take action they consider appropriate to keep the project safe and respectful.
