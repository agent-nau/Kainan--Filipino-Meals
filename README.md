## A week that feels like yours

Whether you're cooking for one or gathering everyone around the table, Kainan helps take the daily “What should we eat?” off your plate.

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="33%" valign="top">
      <h3>01 &nbsp; Plan your week</h3>
      <p>Build a seven-day rhythm of <em>almusal</em>, <em>tanghalian</em>, and <em>hapunan</em>. Set your household size, food preferences, allergies, and budget—then swap meals whenever the week changes.</p>
    </td>
    <td width="33%" valign="top">
      <h3>02 &nbsp; Find a favorite</h3>
      <p>Browse 100 Filipino dishes, explore by ingredient, and discover something new or wonderfully familiar. Each meal shows ingredients scaled to your household.</p>
    </td>
    <td width="33%" valign="top">

## Made for real-life cooking

Keep your pantry in mind. Search dishes by ingredient. Adjust portions for your household. Make the plan your own—one meal at a time.

> **A note on estimates**  
> Grocery prices are rough regional estimates, not live supermarket prices. Calories are estimates, not nutrition advice. “No pork” is a preference, not halal certification.
